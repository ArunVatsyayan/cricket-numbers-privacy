Privacy Policy – Cricket Numbers

Last updated: 13 Feb 2026

Cricket Numbers is an offline-first mobile cricket simulation game. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is (and is not) collected, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

  • No name, email, phone number, or location is collected.
  • No ads SDKs are used.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are used.

The game stores gameplay progress locally on your device using a local save file. This data never leaves your device unless you enable optional cloud save.

2. Optional Cloud Save (Google Play Games)

If you choose to enable cloud save, Cricket Numbers may use Google Play Games Services Snapshots to back up your game progress.

  • This feature is optional and disabled by default.
  • Cloud save is used only to sync your game progress across devices.
  • Data is stored securely by Google according to Google’s privacy policies.

You can disable cloud save at any time from in-game settings or by removing the app’s access in your Google account.

3. Children’s Privacy (Under 13)

Cricket Numbers is suitable for children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from users of any age.

  • No personal data is requested or required to play.
  • No third-party advertising or tracking is present.
  • No chat or user-generated content features exist.

We comply with applicable child privacy laws (including COPPA principles) by not collecting personal data.

4. Data Storage

  • Game progress is stored locally on your device.
  • Optional cloud backups (if enabled) are handled by Google Play Games Services.
  • We do not maintain any external servers for user data.

5. Third-Party Services

Cricket Numbers may integrate with:

  • Google Play Games Services (optional, for cloud save only)

These services are governed by their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing Google’s privacy policy if you use cloud save.

6. Permissions

The app may request minimal permissions required for core functionality (such as storage for saving game progress). No permissions are used to collect personal data.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future if features change. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
