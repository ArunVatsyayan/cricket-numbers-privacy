Privacy Policy — Cricket Numbers

Effective date: 24 October 2026

Cricket Numbers: T20 Manager is a cricket game from Project Rabbit Studio, published on Google Play by Project Rabbit. This policy explains the information used for local gameplay, Google Play Games features, and restoring a permanent Full Game Unlock.

For privacy questions or data requests, contact projectrabbit42@gmail.com.

Local gameplay and settings

The game stores match results, statistics, tournaments, Franchise Career and Player Career progress, created teams and players, and preferences on your device. Preferences include language, sound, graphics, accessibility, batting controls, timing settings, and reminder choices. Names entered for fictional players or teams are part of the saved game; you do not need to enter your real name.

Basic cricket modes work without signing into Google Play Games. An internet connection and Google account verification are required to claim or restore an account-based Full Game Unlock. Play Games platform identity is separate from your primary game account. A verified ownership receipt is also stored on your device so that unlocked gameplay can work offline.

Optional reminders are scheduled on your device. Their local scheduling information is not sent to our ownership service. The game has no in-game chat, advertising SDK, or separate advertising or behavioral-analytics integration.

Google Play Games and cloud progress

On supported Android builds, the game uses Google Play Games Services to check your Play Games identity and provide connected game features. Google may remember your sign-in choice and sign you in automatically according to your Play Games settings. The game receives a Play Games player identifier and can receive your Play Games profile information, such as gamer name and avatar. This is different from asking you to provide a password to Cricket Numbers.

Cloud progress uses a Play Games profile that our service has verified belongs to the same Google account as your primary game identity. The game checks existing cloud progress before allowing uploads. Different existing progress requires an explicit choice; a failed check keeps uploads paused. When the matching profile is connected and that check is complete, game saves can be backed up automatically to Google's Saved Games service. Settings also provides backup and restore actions. A backup contains the saved gameplay information described above, including any names you entered for created teams or players. It is used to restore or synchronize your progress. It is not sent to our Cloudflare ownership database. There is currently no separate in-game cloud-backup on/off switch.

Where Play Games achievements or leaderboards are enabled, achievement status or game scores are sent to Google. Your Play Games profile visibility settings control what other users can see through Google's services. The game does not provide other players with your Google email address through its ownership feature.

Google's Play Games SDK also processes its own gamer-identity, SDK analytics, and diagnostics information. The absence of a separate analytics service in Cricket Numbers does not mean that Google's SDK collects no information. See Google's privacy policy and Play Games SDK data information.

Permanent Full Game Unlock claims

If you select a claim or restore action, the game opens Sign in with Google through Android Credential Manager. It sends Google's signed identity token and a short-lived, single-use challenge over HTTPS to our ownership service, hosted using Cloudflare Workers and D1. The service checks Google's signature, the intended application, issuer, expiry and challenge. Permanent ownership uses Google's stable account identifier, the OpenID subject; it does not use an email address or a device download date as the ownership key.

The game can also request a one-time Play Games server authorization code with the OpenID scope. The service exchanges that code with Google, verifies the game player and checks that its Google subject agrees with your primary account before allowing that profile to synchronize cloud saves. A different or unavailable Play Games profile does not grant another account access to your unlock. Primary Google-account claims can work without an eligible Play Games profile; cloud features then remain unavailable.

The ownership service retains:

  • Your verified Google OpenID subject, stored with a Google-provider prefix.
  • Any same-account Play Games identifiers verified for cloud progress, and whether a cloud link has ever existed. A verified profile/link record can exist even if you have not claimed the Full Game Unlock.
  • A randomly generated game-profile/entitlement identifier and, when you claim an unlock, its original grant time.
  • The association between those identifiers and your permanent Full Game Unlock.

These records let the same verified account restore its unlock after reinstalling the game or changing devices, including after the free offer closes. The launch offer is closed manually by the developer. Ownership records and launch receipts have no automatic expiry; the database is designed to retain ownership records permanently for restoration unless you explicitly delete the game account data.

Standard Google sign-in can supply email, name and profile-image information inside its identity response. The service processes the token in memory and does not retain those standard profile fields in the ownership database. It never asks you to send a Google password to Cricket Numbers, and does not collect contacts, postal addresses or payment-card information for claims. Identity tokens, one-time codes and exchanged access tokens are used for the immediate verification and are not stored in our database or deliberately written to application logs. An ownership receipt contains the verified primary account identifier, entitlement ID, grant time and information needed to verify the grant. It is cryptographically signed and cached locally.

Clearing app data or uninstalling the game does not delete the server ownership record. Removing the game's Google access or deleting Google-held game data does not automatically notify our ownership database to erase its record. Deleting the Google account used to claim can affect the ability to restore its unlock. A Play Games profile change can affect cloud progress and may require verifying the matching profile again.

Network security and abuse prevention

Connecting to the ownership service necessarily exposes your network IP address and request metadata to the hosting provider. For claim, restore and account-deletion requests, the Worker uses secret-keyed hashes of the IP address and verified player ID for short-lived rate limiting. The database stores those hashes, the current minute window, and a request count. These are security request counters, not a download total, claim total, or limit on how many people may own the game.

Expired rate-limit rows become eligible for deletion after approximately two to three minutes and are removed by the scheduled hourly cleanup. With normal scheduling, an inactive row can therefore remain for roughly an hour beyond that short window. Repeated activity refreshes its window. Cleanup delays or service outages can extend retention. Public offer-status polling uses a bounded temporary in-memory IP map rather than durable ownership records.

Sign-in challenges expire after five minutes and are single-use. Used challenges are consumed; unused expired challenge records are removed by scheduled cleanup, which can be delayed by outages. They are security verification state, not ownership counts.

The Worker does not deliberately log request bodies, authorization codes, identity tokens, access tokens, or upstream error bodies. Cloudflare and Google may independently process IP addresses, security, operational, and diagnostic metadata under their own service policies. Disabling Worker application logging does not establish that those providers keep no platform logs.

The game uses HTTPS for account-service traffic and Google's connected services. Signed ownership receipts prevent an ordinary local setting from creating an entitlement. They do not make the associated account identifiers anonymous.

Service providers and other disclosures

Google provides Play Games authentication and connected game services. Cloudflare hosts our ownership verification code and database. They process information needed to deliver those services. Provider processing may take place in the countries where their services operate. Their policies also describe their own handling of data: Google privacy policy and Cloudflare privacy policy.

We do not sell gameplay or ownership information, use it for advertising profiles, or publish the ownership database. If you choose to export a scorecard or copy and send a challenge code, the information in that item is shared through the destination you select. This is separate from the ownership service.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include in the message. That correspondence is used to respond to your request. Do not send Google passwords, one-time authorization codes, or other account credentials in support messages.

Your choices and data requests

You can continue using basic cricket modes without an account-based claim. You can manage the game's connection and your profile visibility through your Google account and Play Games settings. Revoking access may disable connected features and restoration until you authorize them again. Clearing the game's app data removes local saves, preferences, and locally cached receipts; it does not remove Google's cloud data or our ownership record.

Google provides instructions to delete Play Games data or a Play Games profile. Google's deletion controls apply to Google's records; they do not automatically delete developer-held ownership information.

To request deletion from inside the game, open Settings → Delete game account data, sign into the Google account concerned if required, and review the confirmation. The game first verifies the primary Google account and the account service. If that account has ever had a verified Play Games/cloud link, the game requires a fresh matching profile, pauses uploads and attempts to delete all this game's cloud-save snapshots. It does not delete another profile's saves. An account that the server confirms has never had such a link can delete its game profile/ownership without a Play Games snapshot step. It requires native deletion acknowledgements and a successful forced listing showing no remaining snapshots before requesting deletion of the account's server profile, ownership and verified cloud links. It also checks that the final Google authorization still belongs to the account you confirmed. If matching cloud access is unavailable, the game directs you to the support/provider controls instead of falsely reporting all data deleted. If a required step cannot be confirmed, the game tells you what remains unconfirmed; some cloud saves may already have been removed. Local ownership is not cleared on an unconfirmed server result.

Confirmed deletion removes the active ownership record and this device's corresponding cached receipts, and disconnects Google's connected features in this game until you explicitly sign in again. Local matches and Career progress stay on this device. Deleting the unlock can prevent future restoration, and you may be unable to claim it again after the free offer closes. Reconnecting does not automatically create a new free claim. This action does not delete your Google account, your Play Games profile, or Google-held achievements; manage those separately using Google's controls.

The server retains a random deleted entitlement ID and deletion time for 37 days to prevent recovery of an old backup from restoring a deleted account. This deletion ledger does not store the Google player ID, email, authorization token or IP address. Historical provider copies and protected operator backups are separate from active records and are not instantly erased by the account endpoint. The operating procedure limits operator backups to 30 days and requires replaying deletion decisions before serving any recovered copy.

You can submit a deletion request without reinstalling the game at our account-deletion page. For a question or a deletion request outside the app, email projectrabbit42@gmail.com with the subject Cricket Numbers privacy request. Do not send a password. We may need information sufficient to verify the account concerned. Removing the app or revoking its Google access alone does not constitute this request.

Children and connected features

Cricket Numbers is intended for teenage and adult cricket fans aged 13 and above, and is not directed at children under 13. The game does not include advertising or in-game chat. Google-connected features are available only to accounts eligible under Google's age, country and supervision requirements; parental approval may be needed. An intended audience of 13+ does not guarantee that every player can use connected services. If you are a parent or guardian with a question or deletion request about a child's information, contact projectrabbit42@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

Changes to the game's data practices will be reflected in this policy and, where appropriate, in the game before the changed use begins. The effective date identifies the published version.
