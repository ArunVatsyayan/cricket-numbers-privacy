Cricket Numbers

Cricket Numbers: T20 Manager is a cricket simulation for teenage and adult cricket fans. Lead a fictional club through Franchise Career, follow a created or selected cricketer through Player Career, or play individual cricket matches.

Every ball has a reason

Choose Classic batting or six named shots, judge delivery timing, set bowling and field tactics, and inspect the match maths. Teams and players are fictional. Features depend on the installed game version.

Offline play and optional connected features

Basic cricket modes work offline. Eligible players can connect Google Play Games for cloud progress and an account-linked Full Game Unlock. Claiming and restoring an unlock uses a verified Play Games identity and our Cloudflare ownership service. Existing owners can restore after an offer closes.

Read the privacy policy before connecting. You can request deletion of game account data without reinstalling.
