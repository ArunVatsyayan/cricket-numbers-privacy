Request account deletion — Cricket Numbers

This page is for Cricket Numbers: T20 Manager, published by Project Rabbit / Project Rabbit Studio.

Deleting account data removes its permanent Full Game Unlock. You may be unable to claim it again after the free offer closes. Uninstalling alone does not delete ownership records.

Send a request without installing the game

Email projectrabbit42@gmail.com with the subject Cricket Numbers privacy request. State that you want your game account and associated data deleted. Include your Play Games gamer name and player ID if you already have it; you can submit the request if you do not know the ID. Explain whether you also want help deleting Google-held cloud progress.

We review requests individually and reply with any information needed to identify and verify the account concerned. You do not need to reinstall the app to submit a request. A public gamer name alone is not proof that you own an account. Do not send passwords, one-time authorization codes, Google tokens or identity documents in your initial email.

Delete inside the game

In a connected 2.0 build, open Settings → Delete game account data, verify the Play Games account, and read the confirmation. The game checks the account, deletes its game cloud-save snapshots, and then requests removal of its ownership record. If a step cannot be confirmed, it reports the failure; partial cloud deletion may already have occurred.

What is deleted and retained

  • Confirmed account deletion removes the active Full Game Unlock, verified player-ID aliases and corresponding account rate-limit hashes from our service.
  • The in-game flow removes this game's Google cloud-save snapshots before deleting ownership. Local matches and Career progress stay on the device, and the in-game Google connection is disabled until you reconnect explicitly.
  • A random deleted-entitlement ID and deletion time remain for 37 days to prevent old backup recovery from restoring deleted ownership. This ledger does not contain the Google player ID, email address, tokens or IP address.
  • Protected operator backups have a maximum 30-day operating retention; recovered copies must apply deletion decisions before serving users. Cloudflare's provider recovery history is separate from active records.
  • This does not delete your Google account, Play Games profile or achievements. Use Google's game-data controls for Google-held profile and game data. Revoking Google access alone does not delete developer-held ownership.

See the privacy policy's data-request section for the full scope and retention details.
